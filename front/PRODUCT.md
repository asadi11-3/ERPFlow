# ERPFlow product design context

## Register

product

## Users

Sales, approvers, administrators, and service staff use the web client at work to manage sales orders, approvals, machine records, and service jobcards. They need to scan records, trace related equipment and customers, and act on the next valid step.

## Product Purpose

ERPFlow connects order creation and approval with equipment delivery and service. Success means staff can find a record, understand its status and related data, and complete supported workflows without guessing or entering fabricated information.

## Brand Personality

Calm, clear, operational. The interface should feel familiar to staff who work through lists and forms throughout the day.

## Anti-references

Decorative dashboards, gratuitous KPI cards, gradients, and attention-grabbing motion. Avoid inventing business data, steps, or features that the API cannot support.

## Design Principles

1. Lead with the work: show records, status, and available actions before secondary summary.
2. Keep navigation and controls predictable across orders, assets, and service.
3. Make data relationships and server-sourced values explicit.
4. Use concise copy and useful loading, empty, validation, and error states.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keep the app's established baseline: labeled controls, keyboard and focus support, sufficient contrast, and reduced-motion accommodation.
